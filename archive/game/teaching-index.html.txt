        <desc id="dm-desc">Cover as many squares as you can. Choose a board size and difficulty, then place your current piece on empty squares. Every piece covers four squares. You can see the next three pieces. Shapes and orientations are fixed by the starting seed. Easy gives three rerolls, Medium two, Hard one, and Very hard none. Click Reroll pieces or press R to replace the current and next three pieces; held pieces stay unchanged. Hover or focus any empty square that the piece could cover to preview a valid placement, then click or press Enter to place the preview. Keep up to two pieces on hold. Click a hold slot, or press H for slot 1 or J for slot 2, to store the current piece or swap it with that slot. You can hold or swap once per placement across both slots. Placed pieces show their draw numbers, matching the optimal offline solution. Each rerolled piece is a new numbered draw. The game ends when the current piece cannot fit and neither a permitted hold nor a remaining reroll can help. Compare your final layout with the optimal offline packing from every piece you have seen, including held pieces, previews and discarded rerolls. Your score is the percentage of the optimal number of squares that you covered. New game lets you choose a size, difficulty and fresh seed; Reset replays the same original start with both hold slots empty and all rerolls restored.</desc>
